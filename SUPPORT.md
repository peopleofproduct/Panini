# Support Project Panini

**Panini is free forever.** Apache-2.0–licensed, no paid tiers, no paywalled features, no telemetry, no "open core" bait. That will not change.

Everything below is optional support — it funds maintenance, the bank-grammar corpus, and the OCR/benchmark roadmap. Not the code.

## Why support?

Independent market data (see [`future-roadmap-strategy-doc.md`](./future-roadmap-strategy-doc.md) §14) puts Indian lender/fintech spend on statement-parsing and financial-data plumbing at roughly **₹600 Cr+/year**. Panini makes the digital-format majority of that free.

If Panini replaced even a fraction of your vendor fees — ₹10 L/yr for a small fintech, ₹1 Cr+/yr for a mid-size lender — consider routing a small share of that saving back. It keeps the corpus growing and the project maintained.

## For individuals

Any amount via the UPI ID above. ₹100 or ₹10,000 — it all funds real work: new bank layouts, OCR corpora, benchmarks.

## For fintechs, NBFCs, and lenders

If your org uses Panini in production or wants a closer relationship:

- **Org sponsorship** — a recurring annual contribution sized to what Panini saved you (even 1–5% of a displaced vendor contract is meaningful). Get a backer/logo mention in the README and priority triage on issues you file.
- **Partnership / pilot** — help integrating Panini, contributing private-bank grammars, or collaborating on public benchmarks → open a [Discussion](https://github.com/peopleofproduct/Panini/discussions) or reach out via the maintainer's profile.

## What sponsorship is **not**

- It does **not** buy roadmap control, SLAs, or proprietary features.
- It does **not** create a support contract — issues and discussions remain public and best-effort.
- It does **not** change the license or add any form of paid access. Ever.

## Non-financial support is equally valuable

- Star the repo, share it with a lending/fintech team
- Contribute bank grammars, layout JSONs, or corpus fixtures (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md))
- File issues with real-world statement formats we don't yet cover
- Try the [playground](https://peopleofproduct.github.io/Panini/) and report what breaks
